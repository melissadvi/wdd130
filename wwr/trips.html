Our Trips

From a calm family float to a full-day adrenaline run, we have a river adventure for every level. Every trip includes safety gear, an expert guide and a lot of fun

Gentle Float

Our most thrilling trip: a full day of big waves and technical rapids, with a riverside lunch to recharge.
